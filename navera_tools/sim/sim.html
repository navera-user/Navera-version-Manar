NAVERA nodes—
Real: NAVERA's GPS driver, day vision (DINOv2), night vision (STHN), celestial (astrometry.net), arbiter, vehicle output and web view, and the ArduPilot autopilot. Simulated: the world, the GPS receiver, the IMU (with BNO085-class attitude errors) and clock, the camera pictures — cut from the same satellite map vision searches, so vision looks better here than with a real camera (other day, season, angle, sensor; real thermal differs from grey imagery) — and the star images, drawn from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue for a simulated night sky.
