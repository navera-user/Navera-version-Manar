MISSION BRIEF — terminal training
=================================

Your NAVERA computer has just come back from two test flights.
All the files of those flights were dumped, in a mess, into the folder "inbox".

Your job, as the new ground engineer:

  1. Build a clean mission folder:   mission/photos   mission/logs   mission/docs
  2. Put every photo (.jpg) in mission/photos
  3. Put every flight log (.log) in mission/logs
  4. Move the pilot's notes to mission/docs and call them notes.txt
  5. Delete the temporary files (.tmp) and the empty inbox folder
  6. Copy NAVERA's GPS settings file into mission/docs, for the record
  7. Explore your NAVERA copy and answer the questions in answers.py

This brief is exactly 20 lines long (check it with: wc -l mission_brief.txt).
You only need the terminal for all of it.

Good luck, engineer!
