Lesson 19 dataset.

REAL (Insta360 ONE R, USB webcam mode, MJPG 1920x1080, NAVERA bench 2026-10-06):
frames/frame_000..029.jpg  30 consecutive frames saved during NAVERA's lens calibration.
                           Top half = top lens (the one NAVERA uses). Bottom half = bottom
                           lens: it filmed people, so it is PIXELATED (privacy).
insta360_top.yaml          the real calibration of the top lens (fisheye, 1920x540,
                           latency_s 0.138 measured with navera_tools/bench/camera_latency.py).

SYNTHETIC (generated by make_dataset.py, seeded, exact truth — not camera measurements):
grab_times.csv             replay rhythm for the 30 frames (28.8 fps + jitter).
grab_log.csv               60 s of grab times, nominal 28.8 fps, jitter, drops, one stall.
flashes.csv                simulated latency bench: when the program turned the screen white.
flash_frames.csv           simulated latency bench: grab time and centre brightness of each frame.
                           Simulator values: screen delay 20 ms, latency 105 ms, exposure 10 ms.
turn_imu.csv               50 Hz attitude (w,x,y,z) of a drone turning near Sousse, with a dropout.
turn_frames.csv            frames of that flight: grab time + TRUE attitude at the exposure
                           (exposure = grab - 0.138 s exactly).
