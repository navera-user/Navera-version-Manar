# Mission 18 — three synthetic drone photos

Rendered for the course from OpenStreetMap data of Sousse, Tunisia (NAVERA's test area). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL.

Ideal pinhole camera: 960 × 540 px, horizontal field of view 90°, principal point at the image centre, flat ground. Each photo shows a 12 m × 12 m orange landing pad lying on the ground.
