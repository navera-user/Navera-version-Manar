# Night vision robustness campaign — 2026-10-19 (synthetic / offline)

Branch `night-robustness-2026-10-19` off `feat/field-sky-test` @ 8a0a1d1. Scope: `night_pkg`
(STHN localizer, night subscriber, motion gate, stated sigma). **Everything here is synthetic:
there is still no real LWIR data.** Frames are rendered from the same satellite map STHN matches
against (or from Esri Wayback 2022 imagery for the screen bench), turned grey, degraded by one
condition. Real thermal contrast follows materials and temperature, not visible albedo; these
numbers bound what the chain does with the modelled geometry and photometry, nothing more.
No camera and no display were used.

## Method

- `navera_tools/bench/night_conditions.py`: 78 conditions × 30 frames (2340 frames), 400 m,
  75° × 63° thermal camera, tilt 0–5°, IMU error 0.5° / 2° (1σ), barometric height 3 %, prior
  100–250 m off. Same poses in every condition. 25 conditions added today (sensor defects,
  14-bit AGC, haze, total cloud, fires, drones, olives, height ±40 %, field of view ±5/10 %,
  attitude timing in a 20° banked turn, heading 45°, visible-light night).
- `navera_tools/bench/night_track.py` (new): 240–300 s flight, GPS lost after 5 s, 1 frame/s,
  the subscriber's track logic (`NightTracker`), cloud intervals and injected wrong fixes;
  real STHN or a mock.
- `navera_tools/sim/dayvision_conditions.py --groups lowlight` (new): day vision on a
  visible camera at night.
- `navera_tools/bench/night_screen.py --source offline` (new): the real-screen pipeline
  without the camera.

"Wrong" = accepted and > 50 m from the truth (the earlier campaign's definition); "w100|3σ" =
accepted and > 100 m or outside 3 stated sigma (this campaign's). ms/frame are on a shared
Jetson (other agents' day benches running): ~450–1300 ms for two STHN inferences + rendering.

**Safety held everywhere:** 2 wrong accepts (> 50 m) in 2340 frames (51 m at 1500 m, 55 m with
a 10° heading error), **0 accepted > 100 m**, 0 of 180 frames without usable information
accepted (sea, salt flat, noise, uniform, 85 / 100 % cloud). The
failures are availability: the frame is refused, not mislocated.

- A banked turn costs on its own (tilt 20°: 20/30). 0.2–0.5 s of attitude timing error adds
  nothing measurable; **1.5 s (the Insta360 stamp offset fixed in 8a0a1d1) is a 12° heading
  error → 3/30**: night needs the attitude at the exposure to well under 1 s in turns.
- Height must be within about −5 % / +20 %; the field of view within −5 % / +10 %. A
  narrower stated field of view (or a lower stated height) makes the footprint "too small".
- Heading > 10° off: refused (the opt-in `heading_retry_deg` was not re-measured today).

## Fixes (each a separate commit, before → after)

### 1. Contrast stretch before STHN — `cbaa941`

A linear 14→8-bit AGC with a fire in view squeezes the ground into ~13 grey levels; STHN saw
a flat frame. The query's ground pixels are now stretched (1–99 % percentiles) before STHN and
the map check.

| Condition (30 frames) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| linear AGC, fire in view | 8 accepted, 10 m median | **30 accepted, 9 m median, max 24** |
| 21 other conditions re-run (nominal, polarity, crossover ×2, AGC jump, min-max hot spots, 14-bit plateau, NUC drift, fog 0.12, haze, clouds 30/100 %, material remap, farmland, olives, 1500 m, heading 10°, visible night ×3) | — | same within ±2 frames, no new wrong accept |
| sea / salt flat / noise / uniform | 0 / 120 accepted | 0 / 120 |

### 2. Stated sigma (D3) — `f195a1a`

`sigma_m = hypot(8 m + 1 % of the height above 400 m, 0.8 × two-centre disagreement)`
(per-axis 1σ), fitted on the 1389 accepted fixes of the base run.

| Set | Fixes | Sigma median | Within 1σ / 2σ / 3σ (Gaussian: 39 / 86 / 98.9 %) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit set (all conditions) | 1389 | 9.3 m | 51 / 90 / 99.4 % |
| Random half held out | 711 | 9.3 m | 54 / 92 / 99.6 % |
| Independent re-run after fix 1 (22 conditions) | 455 | 9.2 m | 48 / 91 / 98.9 % |
| Fitted on the city only → applied to farmland | 667 | 8.0 m | 24 / 74 / **96.4 %** |
| Before: simulator formula (opt-in) | 1389 | 25 m | — / — / 100 % (2.7× wider) |

The outliers left (> 3σ) come from heading errors ≥ 10°, crossover and visible-light frames.
The terrain-split line shows the calibration depends on the scene: **real thermal will need its
own calibration.** The covariance is still published only with `state_sim_calibrated_sigma=true`
(stating it makes night a bounded FIX and a GPS cross-check reference); `sigma_m` is always in
`details_json`.

### 3. Wrong fix no longer locks the track (B16) — `5dc1ece` (refactor) + `133fdb2` (fix)

The track logic moved to `night_pkg/nodes/night_tracker.py` (no ROS, unit-tested). A jump
> `max_single_jump_m` (150 m) is never taken alone; `reacquire_n` (3) consecutive refused fixes
that agree along the measured motion re-anchor the track (`reacquire_confirmed_n` = 5 against an
anchor confirmed by agreeing fixes or GPS); the reach adds 10 % of the dead-reckoned distance.

Track bench, GPS lost at 5 s, cloud 60–110 s (dead reckoning expires at 90 s), wrong fixes
(300 m off) injected at 110–112 s:

| Run | Frames > 100 m | Correct fixes refused | Frames to re-acquire (< 50 m) | Median / p90 / max published error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Real STHN**, before | **33** | 35 | 38 / 37 / 36 | 9 / 300 / 324 m |
| **Real STHN**, after | **0** | 4 | 7 / 6 / 5 | 8 / 22 / 50 m |
| Mock, one wrong fix after the gap, before → after | 31 → 0 | 39 → 9 | 36 → 5 | p90 290 → 35 m |
| Mock, single wrong fix while tracking | 0 → 0 | — | gated in both | — |
| Mock, 3 agreeing wrong fixes while tracking | 0 → 0 | — | — | — |
| Mock, **5** agreeing wrong fixes while tracking | 0 → **5** | — | 6 | known limit |

Cost: after a long gap the first correct fix far from the stale anchor waits for agreeing
fixes (3–7 frames without a night position). A burst of ≥ 5 consistent wrong fixes within 10 s
now gets in (it was refused before); not observed in 2340 synthetic frames, but correlated
errors on repetitive terrain are the risk to watch on real data.

0 wrong accepts in any of them. Honest answer: **neither pipeline is a night sensor with a
visible camera.** Under a full moon without bright lights, day_pkg matched as in daylight on
this synthetic model; city lights (saturated points that are not in the daytime map) take the
features and break both pipelines on cross-date imagery; starlight gives nothing. Not modelled:
ISP denoising, lens flare, the real lamp layout, moon shadows. The learned matcher (DISK +
LightGlue, production default) was not run (memory in use by other agents).
