# Day vision robustness — simulation / offline campaign, 2026-10-19

Branch `test/day-robustness-2026-10-19` (from `feat/field-sky-test` 8a0a1d1). Scope: day_pkg
(LocalMatcher / LearnedMatcher, VisionNavigator, day_subscriber gates), the bench tool
screen_flight.py. **Everything below is simulation or an offline replay** unless marked
"real camera". No camera and no screen were used (the lead's live bench was running).

## How it was measured

* `navera_tools/sim/day_robustness.py` — single frames rendered from map imagery at a known
  position / height / attitude (renderer of `dayvision_conditions.py`), degraded, straightened
  with an attitude carrying the tested error, located by the **production matcher**
  (LearnedMatcher: DISK + LightGlue, SIFT fallback) with `hfov_deg` (camera-pose position, as
  the day node does). Camera = the 360 bench's straight view, 1200 x 900, 100 deg.
  * scene **bench**: the screen-bench map (Sousse), query frames from the same source
    (MBTiles z18; z17 above 1000 m) + a fixed camera appearance change — **optimistic** (like
    the screen bench, which shows the map itself);
  * scene **xdate2022**: Esri Wayback 2022 frames vs the ~2025 map — **the realistic case**.
  * WRONG = accepted and > 50 m off. ">3 sigma" = accepted with error > 3 x the matcher's
    `sigma_m` (also checked against the published 30 m floor).
* `navera_tools/sim/day_track_sim.py` — a circling flight with VisionNavigator between frames
  (GPS lost 10 min with cloud gaps; GPS spoof 1-100 m/s with the arbiter's GPS rules modelled).
* `navera_tools/sim/gate_replay.py` — **real camera logs** (today's screen_flight hand-tilt runs,
  campaigns 5/7/8 screen_mission) replayed through the track gate (the matcher is not re-run).
* Orin Nano, GPU shared with nothing heavy except my own job; CPU shared with other agents.

## 1. The finding that was fixed: wrong-but-confident matches forwarded

Real camera, 2026-10-19 hand-tilt run (`tilt2.csv`, SIFT, 1400 m equivalent): 73 accepted
matches 100-654 m off, **38-228 inliers (most >= 100)**, all output as NAVERA's position.
Causes found in the code:

| where | rule before | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `screen_flight.py` (bench) | every accepted match = NAVERA output (sigma 10 m) | no consistency check at all |
| `VisionNavigator.update` (screen_mission, shared rules) | a fix with >= 100 inliers bypassed every gate; a doubted fix confirmed by a 2nd one had no distance limit | strong wrong fixes always believed |
| `day_subscriber` (product) | same strong-inlier bypass; motion gate `30 m/s x dt + 250 m` (margin sized for DINOv2 tiles) | a strong match up to ~250 m + v_max dt off forwarded |

Fixes (separate commits): strong fixes gated at 6 sigma of the prediction (then the normal
two-agreeing-fixes re-acquisition), a kinematic gate `v_max x dt + 3 x hypot(sigma_last,
sigma_fix)` from the last fix (VisionNavigator, `max_speed_mps`), the node's motion-gate margin
from the tracked anchor sigma (never more than the old 250 m), screen_flight routed through
VisionNavigator (`--max-speed`, `--no-gate` for the old rule).

Replay of the real logs through the gate (GPS-lost part; vmax 30 m/s bench, 40 m/s campaigns):

| run | estimator | vision > 50 m | of them USED | right (<= 20 m) rejected | measured output > 50 m | max output error | within 3 sigma |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tilt2 (hand tilt) | raw bench rule | 73 | 73 | 0 | **73** | 654 m | 48/127 |
| tilt2 | VisionNavigator before | 73 | 52 | 1 | 53 | 1613 m | 50/155 |
| tilt2 | **after** | 73 | 8 | 2 | **10** | 299 m | 75/155 |
| tilt (camera still) | raw / before / after | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 9 m | 199/199 |
| campaigns 5/7/8 (2350 lost frames, 2017/2022 imagery) | before | 4 | 2 | 67 | 2 | 135 m | 2215/2219 |
| campaigns 5/7/8 | **after** | 4 | 2 | 67 | 2 | 135 m | 2215/2219 |

No regression on 2350 real lost-phase frames; wrong measured outputs on the hand-tilt run
73 -> 10. **Limit:** the hand-tilt errors were mostly the lens moving near the screen (a real
translation of the camera); no gate can make that run right — after a long rejection streak
a biased track can still be re-acquired (10 frames), and while fixes are refused the carried
output itself drifts (any output > 50 m: 101 -> 96 frames).

## 2. Attitude errors (matrix 1)

Tilt error = error of the attitude used to straighten the frame; yaw error = error of the
heading given to the matcher. bench scene, LearnedMatcher, 30 frames (300 m, 800 m tilt <= 1
deg), 20 (800 m), 10 (1500/2000 m: 10-15 s/frame, cut short to spare memory).

| height | tilt err 0 | 0.1 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 4 deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 m | 30/30, 0.4 m | 30/30, 0.4 | 30/30, 0.4 | 30/30, 0.5 | 30/30, 0.8 | 30/30, 0.4 |
| 800 m | 30/30, 0.6 | 30/30, 0.6 | 30/30, 1.0 | 30/30, 1.6 | - | - |
| 1500 m | 10/10, 1.3 | - | - | 10/10, 1.5 | 10/10, 3.2 | 10/10, 1.4 |
| 2000 m | 10/10, 1.8 | - | - | 10/10, 2.8 | - | - |
| xdate2022 300 m | 30/30, 1.7 (p90 2.4) | - | - | 30/30, 3.0 | 30/30, 5.4 (p90 7.1) | 30/30, **11** (p90 13; **6/30 beyond 3 sigma**) |

| height | yaw err 2 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 45 deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 m | 30/30 | 30/30 | 30/30 | **12/30** | **0/30** | **0/30** |
| 1500 m | - | - | 10/10 | **3/10** | **0/10** | - |
| xdate2022 300 m | - | - | 30/30, 1.7 m | **17/30** | - | - |

**Wrong accepts (> 50 m): 0 in every attitude cell.** Beyond 3 matcher sigma: 0, except
xdate2022 with a 4 deg tilt error (6/30: 11 m median vs a 4 m stated sigma).
The camera-pose position (`refine_with_pose`) removes the tilt error almost entirely on
same-source imagery; on 2022 imagery a 2 deg tilt error still costs ~5 m (h tan 2 deg = 10 m).
Yaw errors beyond ~15 deg lose the fix (DISK/LightGlue not rotation invariant + the 20 deg
heading check) — never a wrong fix. -> heading search (section 8).

## 3. Oblique camera, right attitude (matrix 2)

300 m, camera physically tilted 0-30 deg, straightened with the right attitude: 30/30 every
cell, median 0.4 m, max 0.9 m; 800 m at 20 deg: 20/20, 0.6 m. Without the pose (frame
centre, as screen_flight does): 30/30, 0.6 m (straightening with the right attitude is
enough). Rectification holds to 30 deg in this model (flat ground; buildings do not lean).

## 4. Frame / attitude timing at turn rates (matrix 3)

Residual offset 0-200 ms at 5-30 deg/s, modelled as tilt AND yaw error = rate x offset,
300 m, 30 frames: every cell 30/30, median 0.4-0.7 m, max <= 1.6 m (6 deg at 30 deg/s x
200 ms included). The pose absorbs the tilt part; the yaw part stays under the 15-20 deg
where matching fails. The 360's straight view is cut towards the ground with the attitude,
so a bank angle itself does not tilt the view; only its error does.

## 5. Speed and stream-delay mis-estimate (matrix 4)

300 m, 2 ms exposure, 20 frames: blur up to 80 m/s costs nothing (0.4 m); 8 ms at 80 m/s
also 20/20, 0.4 m. A delay mis-estimate d moves the fix by v x d along track, exactly:

| speed | delay err 0 | +-0.2 s |
|---|---|---|
| 20 m/s | 0.4 m | 4.0 m |
| 40 m/s | 0.4 m | 8.0 m |
| 60 m/s | 0.4 m | 12 m (**17-20/20 beyond 3 matcher sigma**, within 3 x 30 m published) |
| 80 m/s | 0.4 m | 16 m (**20/20 beyond 3 matcher sigma**) |

The matcher's sigma (4 m at >= 100 inliers) does not include the timing error: at >= 60 m/s
a 0.2 s delay error makes every fix "overconfident" against its own sigma (open item O4).

## 9. Long GPS loss and spoofing (track simulation, SIFT on CPU, xdate2022 300 m, 20 m/s)

GPS lost 600 s, 40 s of 95 % cloud every 120 s: 601 frames, 404 matched, 367 used, **0 wrong
used**; output median 2.8 m, p90 24 m, max 45 m; **557/557 outputs within 3 stated sigma**
(propagated 190: median 15 m, max 45 m). But the sigma is **very conservative**: after a 40 s gap

## Open problems (for the problems doc)

* **O1 — learned matcher slow above ~500 m.** `learned_matcher._locate_learned` enlarges the
  frame to the map's scale (s = frame m/px / map m/px: 1.6x at 800 m, 3x at 1500 m) and runs DISK
  in chunks: 2.3-2.6 s/frame at 800 m, 10-15 s/frame at 1500-2000 m (vs 0.35 s at 300 m), process
  RSS up to 5.2 GB at 2000 m. Impact: stale positions, memory pressure on the 8 GB Jetson. Fix:
  match high frames against a coarser map level (z16/z15 chunks) or cap the enlargement (as
  LocalMatcher does: never enlarge, scale keypoints).
* **O2 — published day sigma is a fixed 30 m floor** (`day_subscriber._publish_estimate` uses
  `local_sigma_m`, not the fix's `sigma_m`). Impact: the arbiter's 3-sigma GPS cross-check only
  refuses a spoof after ~90-150 m of drift (sim) although vision is 2-5 m. Fix: publish
  `max(sigma_m, floor)` with a smaller validated floor (~10 m) once the real camera campaign
  confirms the per-inlier sigma.
* **O3 — dead-reckoning sigma very conservative** (VisionNavigator.predict: 0.5 a dt^2 with
  a = 0.5 m/s^2 and 5 deg heading term): after 40 s without a fix sigma 446 m vs 33 m real
  error; output "unavailable" past 300 m. Honest (557/557 within 3 sigma) but costs
  availability. Fix: tune `accel_mps2` per vehicle / use the IMU once wired.
* **O4 — fix sigma ignores timing**: at >= 60 m/s a 0.2 s delay error (12-16 m) is beyond 3 x
  the matcher sigma in 17-20/20 frames. Fix: add v x sigma_delay (e.g. 0.05-0.1 s) to `sigma_m`.
* **O5 — after a wrong fix is accepted its sigma stays small** (4 m with a 300 m error in the
  tilt2 replay; inflating it by the disagreement was tried and made things worse: more wrong
  fixes passed the wider gates). Needs a proper multi-hypothesis / re-acquisition confidence.
* **O6 — screen_flight locates the frame centre** (no `hfov_deg` -> no camera pose): bench
  positions carry h tan(attitude error) (1 deg at 1400 m = 24 m) that the product does not.
  Left as is to keep today's bench baseline comparable; pass `hfov_deg=a.hfov` for product parity.
* **O7 — yaw error > 15-20 deg = no fix** without `local_heading_search`; enable it for IMUs
  without magnetometer (cost 0.5-5 s on the first frame, cheap after).
* **O9 — fix sigma ignores the attitude error**: on 2022 imagery the camera pose removes only
  part of a straightening tilt error (300 m: 1 deg 3.0 m, 2 deg 5.4 m, 4 deg 11 m median, 6/30
  beyond 3 sigma at 4 deg). Today's measured attitude (0.4 deg median, 1.2 deg max while
  turning) stays inside the sigma at 300 m but scales with height. Fix: add
  ~0.5 x h x tan(attitude sigma) to `sigma_m` (attitude sigma from the IMU state).
* **O8 — sea / 2017 imagery**: 0/30 offshore, 13/30 on 8-year-old imagery (no wrong fixes).

## Honest limits

Simulation: flat ground (no relief, buildings do not lean), synthetic appearance changes,
same-source imagery for most cells (optimistic: real frames are 2.5-8.7 m on the bench, not
0.4 m), no lens/rolling shutter model, attitude errors as clean rotations. Frame counts were
cut above 500 m (20 or 10 frames) for time and memory. The gate replay reuses logged match
results (the matcher was not re-run after a different decision). Nothing here was flown.
