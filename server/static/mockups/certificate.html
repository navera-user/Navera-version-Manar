How it works
• When a student passes mission 30 with all 30 missions done, the home page shows 🎓 Get my certificate. They confirm the name to print (it can't change afterwards), then download the PDF or share the verification link.
• The academy signs the certificate's data (number, name, programme, date) with an IRIS private key kept on the Jetson (only the academy can read it). Anyone can check it with the public key — no account needed.
• The teacher page lists the certificates issued, and can revoke one.
