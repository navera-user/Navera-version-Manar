What changes
• Flight: numbered steps — Route → Vehicle → Conditions (Light is set here only, and can be switched during the flight). The vehicle choice leaves the crowded top bar; the route shows distance and time; ⇅ swaps start and destination. The mission-28 scenario stays on top when the mission has one.
• Faults: one card per sensor with its current state as a pill (follows your script too), “Restore everything”, and the last few events right under it so you see NAVERA react.
• Code: two clear steps — which estimator flies, then your flight script — with its own output under it; “API help” lists every sim.… call.
• Results (was “Log”): the outcome of the flight on top, “Save to my_runs/” with a name field, then the log with a filter (events only, or with NAVERA's own messages).
