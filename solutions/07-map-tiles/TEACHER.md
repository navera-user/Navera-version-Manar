- `tiles.py` follows design §2.3 names (`tile_xy`, `tile_to_latlon`, `m_per_px`, `best_zoom(gsd_m, lat, zmin=13, zmax=19)`,
  `read_tile(db, z, x, y)`, `latlon_to_pixel`, `pixel_to_latlon`, `MAP_PATH`, `map_patch(lat, lon, z, w, h)`), plus
  `mercator_y` (TODO 1, for fading) and `open_map(path=None)` (TODO 6). **`read_tile` takes an open connection** (from
  `open_map`), not a path; `map_patch` takes an optional `path=` and opens/closes its own connection; `patch_origin` gives
  the fractional top-left tile of a patch so later missions (08, 21–25, 23's `search_patch`) can convert patch pixels
  with `pixel_to_latlon`. `MAP_PATH` reads `NAVERA_LITE_MAP` (the env name the sandbox sets), not `FLEDGLING_MAP`.
- The SQL-injection "attacker" role of the old brief was replaced by a defensive step (input validation + placeholders
  explained, WEB-4 as the real incident); no attack exercise is included.
- The lesson no longer quotes the 10 GB Esri map's facts (size, estimated_size_mb); "metadata vs measurement" now uses
  the OSM map's `bounds` against its real tile coverage.
- Old `datasets/07-map-tiles/make_dataset.py` deleted (generators must not be in datasets/); `README.md` added there with
  the attribution. `assets/mosaic_pixels.svg`: the Singapore corner value (a lab answer) removed.
