## How the guard plugs into mission 29's judge (the interface)

`GuardedArbiter(arbiter, guard)` needs only `arbiter.receive(est, now_s)` and `arbiter.step(now_s)` → a decision
with `state`, `source`, `estimate`, `reason` (attributes or dict keys). Every estimate goes through
`guard.check(est)` first; the judge then receives a TRUSTED GPS fix unchanged, an IN_DOUBT one with its horizontal
variances raised to the gap² (`doubtful`, so the judge's own `classify` says DEGRADED — NAVERA's documented "accuracy
no better than the gap"), a REFUSED one as `valid=False` ("no position": the judge's eligibility drops GPS at once),
every other source unchanged. The judge needs no change and no hook; its own JumpGate stays (the guard and the
judge's gate use the same settings, so they agree). `spoof_run.py` and `guard_node.py` import `navera_lite.arbiter`;
`spoof.py` does not.
