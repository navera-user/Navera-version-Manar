## Difficulties seen in testing (author's mutant runs, 2026-10-15)

17 wrong-but-plausible versions, each caught with a specific message: nearest-sample percentile, unsorted
percentile, position n·q instead of (n − 1)·q, flat distance without cos(lat), NaN rows kept, RMSE = std,
availability from the state only, gap without dt_s, permutation instead of resampling, wrap-around blocks, blocks
not cut to n, paired with separate indices, Wilson = naive interval, a new generator per trial, coverage as a count,
baseline frozen at the truth, baseline frozen at the last (vision) row. The runner's `test` lines show the wrong
values the lab's hints quote (verified: 5 / 2 for percentile, rmse 3.0, (0.800, 9.000), 932, `nan`).
Expected student difficulties: `rng.integers` upper bound excluded; passing `np.median` without calling it (🧰
"functions are values"); confusing the jam rows with the rows that have a position (the runner pairs them).
